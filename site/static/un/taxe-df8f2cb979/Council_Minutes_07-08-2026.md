## Appendix — Minutes Format & Future Sessions

**The Minute** (house standard, going forward):
- One line per vote, override, or Directive
- Who decided, what was decided, who dissented (if any)
- No narrative narration; just record

**Full Minutes** (as above):
- Produced after major sessions (handovers, major votes, Work completions)
- Appended to `Council_Minutes.md` in the project repo
- Keeper-approved before being added to the record

**Voice:** Written in the past tense, formal register, signed by the Keeper or their proxy (Fen).
